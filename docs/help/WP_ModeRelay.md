# WP Mode Relay

Watches the nodes wired into it and, when what they do changes, mutes, bypasses or runs the nodes of the WP Node Group its output goes into. Frontend only: it never runs and is left out of the prompt.

## On the node

- **watch dots (in)**: Wire the nodes to watch. A new free dot appears as you wire.
- **drives (out)**: Wire it into a WP Node Group: the relay sets that group's nodes.
- **All muted**: What to send when every watched node is muted: Run, Mute, Bypass or nothing (—).
- **All bypassed**: What to send when every watched node is bypassed.
- **Any runs**: What to send when at least one watched node runs.
