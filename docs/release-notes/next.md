### Features

- **Pick which images go on to the upscaler with WP Image Filter.** Put the new node between your first KSampler and the upscaler. When a run reaches it, a picker opens with every image (grouped by frame when a Context Loop made them). Click the ones you want, press **Space** to look closer, and **Enter** to send them on. Only the picked images continue, and their latent, masks, positive and negative conditioning, prompt texts and Context come along with them, so the upscaler gets each image's own prompt. Two **extra** slots carry anything else that should follow the picks. **Keep all** lets everything through and **Stop branch** stops what comes after the node without failing the run. On the node you choose whether it pauses every run, reuses your last picks, or lets everything pass, a timeout, and whether picks keep their batches or go on one image per item. The picker can be tucked away to look at the canvas and keeps your picks until you open it again. While it waits, a short chime plays and the tab title starts with **● Pick images**, so you notice from another tab (new settings: **Image Filter sound**, on by default, and **Image Filter desktop notification**, off by default). No schema change.
- **Refine your picks before they reach the upscaler.** In the Image Filter zoom, a **Refine** panel lets you edit the frame's positive and negative prompt and paint a mask on the image. An edited prompt replaces the text output, and with a CLIP wired into the node's new optional **clip** input the conditioning is re-encoded to match. A painted mask goes to the **masks** output, so an inpainting upscaler can work on just that area.
- **The Image Filter picker knows your Context Loop.** Frames are labelled with the options your sweep pinned (hover for the seed and prompt), a sweep opens as a grid where you choose which wildcards go on rows and columns and the rest split into small grids (click a row, column or group name to pick all of it), **C** compares two images with a slider, and the picker opens zoomed if you left it zoomed. **Send to Loop** writes your picks back to the loop, locking the kept frames' seeds and bypassing the rest, so the next run repeats only what you liked.

### Fixes

- **Sweep combinations can clear a wildcard's options in one click.** Next to **all** and **enabled only** there is now **none**, so you can untick everything and pick just the few options you want instead of unticking them one by one. A wildcard with nothing ticked sweeps nothing until you tick one.
- **The loop's count is locked while a sweep is on.** The sweep decides how many frames run, so the count field now greys out and shows the sweep's frame count instead of looking editable.
