### Features

- **Pick which images go on to the upscaler with WP Image Filter.** Put the new node between your first KSampler and the upscaler. When a run reaches it, a picker opens with every image (grouped by frame when a Context Loop made them). Click the ones you want, press **Space** to look closer, and **Enter** to send them on. Only the picked images continue, and their latent, masks, positive and negative conditioning, prompt texts and Context come along with them, so the upscaler gets each image's own prompt. Two **extra** slots carry anything else that should follow the picks. **Keep all** lets everything through and **Stop branch** stops what comes after the node without failing the run. On the node you choose whether it pauses every run, reuses your last picks, or lets everything pass, what happens when nothing is picked, a timeout, and whether picks keep their batches or go on one image per item. The picker can be tucked away to look at the canvas and keeps your picks until you open it again, and a short chime tells you it is waiting when you are in another tab (turn it off with the new **Image Filter sound** setting). No schema change.

### Fixes

- **Sweep combinations can clear a wildcard's options in one click.** Next to **all** and **enabled only** there is now **none**, so you can untick everything and pick just the few options you want instead of unticking them one by one. A wildcard with nothing ticked sweeps nothing until you tick one.
- **The loop's count is locked while a sweep is on.** The sweep decides how many frames run, so the count field now greys out and shows the sweep's frame count instead of looking editable.
